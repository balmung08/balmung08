## 👋 Hey there! 
I'm currently a PhD student at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)**,
working on **motion planning and collaborative intelligence for continuum robots**.

---

### 🔬 Research Overview

My research lies at the intersection of **robotics**, **motion planning**, **control**, and **embodied intelligence**.

Previously, my work focused on **mobile robot motion planning, control, and real-world autonomous systems**, with an emphasis on enabling robots to operate reliably in complex and unstructured environments.

My current research is shifting toward **continuum surgical robots**, especially **motion planning**, **multi-robot collaboration**, and **autonomous decision-making in constrained environments**.

---

### 🧭 Research Philosophy

I believe robotics should not only **extend human capability**,
but also **liberate humans from repetitive and hazardous work**, moving toward truly **autonomous, self-evolving systems**.

My long-term goal is to **bridge rigorous mathematical modeling** with **embodied intelligence**,
enabling robots to **understand, adapt, and act independently** in dynamic environments.
